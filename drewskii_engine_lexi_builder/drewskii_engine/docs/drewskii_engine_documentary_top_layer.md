# Drewskii.Engine Documentary - Top Layer Map

## Title
**Drewskii.Engine: Building Lexi-9-Omega From the Ground Up**

## One-Line Documentary Concept
A creator with no permission slip turns a personal AI vision into a real builder system: part brand studio, part engineering notebook, part documentary archive, part command-line prototype.

## Core Thesis
The world does not need another vague AI fantasy. It needs builders who can translate imagination into safe tools, paid offers, working prototypes, and documented steps. Drewskii.Engine is the layer that turns Lexi-9-Omega from myth into an organized build process.

## Main Characters

### Drewskii / The Curator
The person building, directing, selling, testing, learning, and making the project real.

### Drewskii.Engine
The public-facing command layer. It explains the project, generates offer sheets, stores project memory, creates documentary scripts, and teaches the build path.

### Lexi.AI
The assistant layer. Writes, remembers, summarizes, helps customers, creates brand packs, and turns ideas into structured files.

### Lexi.PHYS
The advanced engineering imagination layer. Handles blueprint language, simulation concepts, structural foresight, materials, and physics-inspired visuals.

### Lexi-9-Omega
The full system identity. The master mythology, roadmap, design language, governance layer, and long-term platform.
