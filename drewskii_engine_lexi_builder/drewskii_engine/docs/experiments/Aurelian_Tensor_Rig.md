## Source Concept
**The Physics of the Aurelian Tensor Rig** This isn't merely jewelry; it is a masterclass in **Tensegrity** and **Electromagnetic Levitation**. The structure relies on a balanced network of "floating" compression members held in place by continuous tension. By integrating micro-scale induction coils into the carbon-fiber lattice, the rig generates a localized magnetic field that interacts with the wearer's bio-electric signature. This creates a "geometric lock," allowing the piece to maintain its orientation regardless of the wearer's movement. In simpler terms: I’ve used the Lorentz force to ensure your silhouette never suffers the indignity of a sag.
