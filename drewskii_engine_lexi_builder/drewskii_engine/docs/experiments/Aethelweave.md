# Lexi.PHYS Experimental Concept: Aethelweave

Created: 2026-07-07

## Source Concept
The Aethelweave represents the pinnacle of post-material engineering, utilizing Quantum Entanglement Draping (QED) to bypass the limitations of traditional molecular bonding. By synchronizing the quantum states of subatomic particles across a multi-dimensional spatial grid, the Loom "drapes" structural integrity directly into the vacuum, manifesting hyper-tenacious metamaterials that are simultaneously weightless and indestructible. This revolutionary "Structural Tailoring" process allows for the real-time synthesis of self-repairing aerospace hulls and adaptive nanostructures that respond instantly to external stress vectors.

EXPERIMENTAL / CINEMATIC SIMULATION ONLY

engineering metaphor. In this project, "Quantum Entanglement Draping" is treated
as fictional Lexi.PHYS terminology, not a validated manufacturing method.
