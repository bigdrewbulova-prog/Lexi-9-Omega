# Lexi.PHYS Experimental Concept: Bio-Electric Interface

Created: 2026-07-06T20:33:12

## Source Concept
The Physics of the Bio-Electric Interface The secret to the Aurelian Rig is **Impedance Matching**. Your body’s electrical signals are chaotic and high-impedance. The Rig uses a series of **Tensor-Field Converters** to smooth these signals into a coherent stream. By aligning the geometric orientation of the superconducting traces with the natural vector pathways of your nervous system, we minimize "dark noise." Essentially, we are turning your nervous system into a high-speed fiber-optic cable.

## Blocked Claims
Do not claim:
- Literal structure draped into the vacuum
- Weightless and indestructible physical material
- Real-time quantum entanglement manufacturing
- Using a wearer's bio-electric signature as a real control or authentication field
- Turning a real nervous system into a data cable or controllable signal bus
- Body-signal impedance matching as a real medical or neural interface
- Tensor-field converters as real bio-signal conditioning hardware
- Wearable superconducting traces aligned to human neural pathways
- Dark-noise reduction as a validated biological signal treatment
