# Interview Questions

## For Andrew / Curator
1. What was the first moment Lexi felt real to you?  
2. What problem do you want Lexi to solve first?  
3. Why does the world need personal AI builders?  
4. How do you balance the dream with what can be built today?  
5. What would your first paid customer prove?  
6. What does “Curator Access” mean for authorship and control?  
7. When did usefulness become more important than mythology?  
8. What are you unwilling to automate or override on a phone?  
9. What does a good Brand Blueprint Pack feel like when it lands?  
10. If Lexi only does one thing perfectly this year, what is it?

### Follow-ups (optional)
- What almost made you quit the build?  
- Which Lexi layer is hype vs which layer is already shipping?  
- What would you show a skeptic in 60 seconds?

## For Customers
1. What did your brand look like before?  
2. What did Lexi help you clarify?  
3. Would you pay for a finished blueprint pack?  
4. What made the result feel professional?  
5. What would you want in a v2 pack (logo set, ad kit, voice kit)?  
6. How fast did you need the deliverable?  
7. Would you recommend this to another creator or small business?
