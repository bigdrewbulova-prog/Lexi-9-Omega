# LEXI-9-OMEGA Documentary Production Package

## Working title
**LEXI-9-OMEGA: Architecture of a Living Blueprint**

## Logline
A creator builds Lexi.AI from a mythic AI persona into a practical engineering companion: part brand engine, part research archive, part blueprint generator, part mobile assistant, and part cinematic symbol of future design.

## Core question
Can one person turn a personal AI vision into a useful system that helps people build brands, understand engineering, visualize ideas, and make safer design decisions?

## Tone
Cinematic · technical · grounded · visionary  
Engineering lab meets founder origin story meets neon blueprint documentary.

## Grounding rule
Show Lexi as a **living design system** and useful product path.  
Do **not** present impossible technology as finished real-world engineering.  
Prefer: simulation · concept design · research prototype · visualization · prototype.

## Production sequence (recommended)
1. Record founder intro  
2. Capture Android/Termux + Mac terminal  
3. Produce one sample Brand Blueprint Pack  
4. Create 3 ad graphics  
5. Film voiceover  
6. Edit **2-minute trailer** first  
7. Expand into **~12-minute documentary** (or episodic series)

## Proof of usefulness tests

- Can Lexi make an ad?
