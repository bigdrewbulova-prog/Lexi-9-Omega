## TL;DR
Centralized AI concentrates compute, data, and policy. Lexi-9-Omega is a **local-first AI workbench** you can run, inspect, and own: Blueprint Forge deliverables, CORTANA-PHYS model routing with local keys, Autonomy OS event logs + safety stops, and a live evidence-to-action demo.

## The problem
Big Tech monopolizes infrastructure and data. Users rent cognition; exit is painful. You will not beat centralized AI by building another landlord.

## The solution (stack)
1. **Compute** — local/Ollama-first; optional decentralized GPU later (Nosana).
2. **Data** — SQLite + MD/JSON/HTML artifacts on disk; local `cph_` API keys.
3. **Governance** — safety supervisors, event-sourced logs, human approval; sims labeled sim.
