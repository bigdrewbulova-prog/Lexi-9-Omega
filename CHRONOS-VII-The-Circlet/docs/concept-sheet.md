A ring-shaped **story object** that drives a spacetime sandbox: timeline branches, light-cone tilt, entropy, and causality risk — for film, games, and Lexi.PHYS education.

## UI concept layers

1. **Ring silhouette** — static SVG / canvas jewelry form  
2. **Inner band glow** — maps to “field intensity” (slider), not RPM hardware  
3. **Cone overlay** — 2D light-cone wedges at wearer worldline  
4. **Branch lattice** — many-worlds style tree of offsets  
5. **Meters** — entropy, causality risk, shield integrity, erosion  

## Repair / stress states (simulation only)

| State | Visual | Player meaning |
|---|---|---|
| NOMINAL | Acid-green lattice | Safe demo range |
| STRESSED | Amber cones, branch jitter | High tilt or many branches |
| EROSION | Ghost trails of prior paths | Entropy + risk thresholds |
| LOCKOUT | Dim ring, red ledger | Force recalibration narrative |
